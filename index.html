01 — Library

A library that belongs to the listener.

Audacto treats local files as a first-class music library. It's useful on its own, with no streaming service behind it.

Import

Bring what you own.

  • Files and whole folders from the Files app
  • Downloaded, unprotected tracks from the Music app
  • The share sheet and “Open in Audacto”
  • Finder drag-and-drop over USB

Organize

Songs, albums, playlists.

  • One search across title, artist and album
  • Playlists and rule-based smart playlists
  • M3U playlist import and export
  • Ratings and custom artwork

Keep

Built to last.

  • Files copied into the app's private storage
  • Automatic library backups you can restore
  • Missing-file checks with a careful cleanup
  • A library that survives a device restore

02 — Equalizer

Shape it, precisely.

Two equalizers, one signal path. Make broad strokes with the graphic EQ, fine corrections with the parametric EQ, and watch the result on a live post-EQ spectrum.

31

Graphic EQ

Third-octave bands from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.

8

Parametric EQ

A low shelf, six bells and a high shelf, each with its own frequency and gain, plus Q on every bell.

In / Out

Presets

Save, rename and switch presets, and import or export them as files.

Try it · four bells

Drag a filter. With the keyboard, arrows move frequency and gain, Shift takes bigger steps, and Page Up/Down changes Q. The magenta line is the response reflection.

03 — Now Playing

Thirteen ways to watch it play.

Song Cover shows the track's artwork by default. Twelve live visualizers follow the real playback level after the EQ, and any of them can fill the screen as a projector.

05 — Privacy

Nothing to collect.

Audacto has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no networking features. Your library, playlists, presets and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device.
