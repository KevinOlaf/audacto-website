01Importing music

How do I add music?

Tap Import in Library and choose From Files to pick files or whole folders, or From Apple Music to pick tracks from the Music app.

You can also share an audio file to Audacto from another app, open it with “Open in Audacto”, or drag files into Audacto's folder in Finder with your device connected over USB. Audacto imports them the next time it opens.

Which formats are supported?

MP3, AAC and M4A, FLAC, WAV, AIFF and CAF.

Why were some Apple Music tracks not imported?

Audacto can only import music you own that is downloaded to your device. Copy-protected tracks, such as songs available through an Apple Music subscription, and tracks that aren't downloaded can't be imported. The import summary lists them by title so you can see what was skipped.

What happens to duplicates?

Audacto recognizes a file it already has and reports it as already in your library instead of adding it twice.

02Your library

Where are my files stored?

Imported files are copied into Audacto's private storage on your device, so your library keeps working even if you move or delete the originals. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do backups work?

Audacto regularly saves a backup of your library information, such as playlists, ratings and track details, on your device. You can see when the last backup was made in Settings → Library & Storage.

If your library ever can't be opened, Audacto offers Retry Open and, when a usable backup exists, Restore Backup. Restoring replaces library information with the backup. Your audio and artwork files are kept, but a backup can't bring back files that were deleted.

A track shows as missing. What can I do?

A warning icon means the track's file is no longer in Audacto's storage. Open Settings → Library & Storage and tap Recheck Library to update the counts. Remove Missing Entries deletes those entries and their playlist references after you confirm; files that are still present are kept.

Can I move playlists in or out?

Yes. Use Import Playlist on the Playlists screen to bring in an M3U file, and Export Playlist on a playlist's own screen to save it as M3U.

03Playback & EQ

What's the difference between the two equalizers?

The Graphic EQ has 31 fixed bands from 20 Hz to 20 kHz for broad tonal shaping. The Parametric EQ has eight filters (a low shelf, six bells and a high shelf), each with adjustable frequency and gain, plus Q on the bells, for precise corrections. A live spectrum shows the result after the EQ.

Can I share or back up my EQ presets?

Yes. In Manage Presets you can save, rename and delete presets, and import or export them as files.

How do I turn off crossfade?

In Settings, choose Gapless as the transition. Crossfade can be set anywhere from 1 to 12 seconds.

Can I use Audacto with larger text or VoiceOver?

Yes. Audacto supports Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility sizes, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast. If something is hard to use with an accessibility setting, please tell us. We treat it as a bug.

04Requirements

Audacto runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. It works fully offline and doesn't need an account.
