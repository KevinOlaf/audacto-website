Privacy Policy

Nothing to collect.

Audacto is an offline audio player. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no networking features, so there is no personal data for us to collect, share or sell.

In short

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No networking features. Audacto doesn't send your library anywhere.
  • Everything you import or create stays in the app's private storage on your device.

01What stays on your device

To work as a music library, Audacto keeps the following in its private storage on your iPhone or iPad. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Audio files you import. Audacto copies each file you choose into its own storage so your library keeps working if the original moves.
  • Library information. Titles, artists, albums, genres and other details read from your files, plus your ratings, play counts, playlists and smart playlist rules.
  • Artwork. Artwork embedded in your files, and any images you choose as custom artwork.
  • Equalizer presets and settings. Your EQ curves, presets, playback preferences and the last playback position.
  • Library backups. Snapshots of your library information, kept on the device so you can restore it if the library can't be opened. Backups don't contain copies of your audio files.

Deleting a track in Audacto removes its file and library entry. Deleting the app removes everything above from your device.

02Permissions

Audacto asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings.

Media & Apple Music

Used only when you choose to import music you own from the Music app. Audacto imports the tracks you pick that are downloaded to your device and not copy-protected.

Photos

Used only when you choose an image as custom artwork. Audacto copies the selected image into its own storage and doesn't read the rest of your photo library.

Files

Audacto reads only the files and folders you select. Its Documents folder appears in the Files app and in Finder so you can drop in files to import.

03Apple services

Some standard iOS features handle data according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy, not ours:

  • Device backups. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include Audacto's data in that backup.
  • Now Playing. The current track's title, artist and artwork appear on the Lock Screen and in Control Center while you listen.
  • Widgets. If you add Audacto's widgets, the playback status they show is shared on your device through a private app group.
  • App Store. Downloads and any purchases are handled by Apple. We receive only the aggregated reports Apple provides to developers.

Audacto doesn't run a cloud service of its own.

04This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from this site. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement.

05Children

Audacto doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

06Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If Audacto ever adds a feature that sends data off your device, we'll describe it here before it ships.
